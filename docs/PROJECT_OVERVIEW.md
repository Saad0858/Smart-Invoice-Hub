# Project Overview

## Product Name

BillFlow ERP

## Vision

Build a modern GST Billing & Invoice Management System that is easy to use, visually impressive, and scalable for manufacturing businesses.

## Target Customers

* Manufacturers
* Industrial Units
* Traders
* Wholesalers
* MIDC Companies

## Initial Customer

Manufacturing company located in MIDC, Kupwad.

## Business Goals

* Replace manual billing
* Generate GST invoices
* Store billing history
* Improve customer management
* Build a reusable SaaS product

## Prototype Scope

* Authentication
* Dashboard
* Product Management
* Customer Management
* Invoice Generation
* PDF Export
* Invoice History
* Company Settings

## Out of Scope

* Inventory Management
* Accounting
* Payment Gateway
* E-Invoice
* E-Way Bill
* Multi-tenant support (prototype only)
