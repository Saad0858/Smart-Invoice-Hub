# Smart Invoice Hub

## Overview

BillFlow ERP is a modern cloud-based GST Billing & Invoice Management System designed for manufacturers, wholesalers, and small to medium businesses.

The goal of this project is to replace traditional billing software with a fast, intuitive, and modern web application.

The first version is being developed as a prototype for demonstration and customer validation. After validation, the project will evolve into a production-ready multi-tenant SaaS platform.

---

## Objectives

* Modern GST Invoice Generation
* Customer Management
* Product Management
* PDF Invoice Export
* Invoice History
* Sales Dashboard
* Reports & Analytics
* Company Settings
